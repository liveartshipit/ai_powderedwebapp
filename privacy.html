Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Short version: we use Google Analytics, only if you accept it, to see which stories are read. There are no ads, accounts or forms, and we never sell data. You can change your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Who we are

Worksmarto AI News (news.worksmarto.com) is operated by Worksmarto, based in Erode, Tamil Nadu, India. It is part of Worksmarto (worksmarto.com). For the purposes of India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), we act as the Data Fiduciary, and for the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), as the Data Controller, for any personal data processed through this site.

Contact: hello@worksmarto.com.

This policy covers news.worksmarto.com only. Our main website, worksmarto.com, uses analytics, advertising and forms and has its own Privacy Policy, which applies when you visit it.

What we collect

There are no accounts, sign-ups, comments, contact forms, advertising, tracking pixels or social media widgets on this site. The data processing that does happen is described below.

  • Analytics data (only with your consent). If you click “Accept” on the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4, provided by Google LLC, to understand how the site is used. It collects your approximate location (derived from your IP address), browser and device type, the pages you view, the links you click, how long you stay and the website that referred you. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store full IP addresses. We use this only in aggregate, to decide which kinds of stories are useful. If you click “Reject”, or make no choice, Google Analytics stays switched off: it measures nothing and sets no cookies. The Google tag’s code is still downloaded by your browser on each page (so Google can confirm the site is set up), which means Google receives your IP address and browser information, as with our fonts.
  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. (United States). Like any web host, GitHub receives technical information when your browser requests a page, including your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and may keep it in logs for security and reliability. We do not receive or access these logs. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.
  • Web fonts. Our fonts (Inter, Manrope and Space Grotesk) are loaded from Google Fonts, provided by Google LLC (United States). Your browser connects to Google’s servers to download them, so Google receives your IP address and browser information. Google states that Google Fonts does not set cookies. See Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • Theme preference. If you use the light/dark switch, your choice is saved in your own browser’s local storage under the name news-theme. It never leaves your device, is not a cookie, and is not sent to us. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.
  • Sharing. The Share button uses your device’s own share menu or copies the link to your clipboard. Nothing is sent to us.

Cookies and your choice

This site sets cookies only if you accept analytics:

  • _ga and _ga_<ID>: Google Analytics cookies that distinguish visitors and sessions. They are set on news.worksmarto.com only, and expire after up to 2 years.

Your choice itself (accept or reject) is saved in your browser’s local storage under news-consent, so we don’t ask again on every visit. It is not sent to us.

You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw consent, Google Analytics stops and its cookies on this site are removed. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before.

Your choice on this site is separate from any choice you make on worksmarto.com.

Why we use the data, and on what legal basis

We use analytics data to understand which stories and sections readers find useful and to improve the site. Under the GDPR our legal basis is your consent. Under the DPDP Act, we rely on your consent given through the cookie banner. Technical data processed by our host and font provider is handled on the basis of our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website.

Advertising

This site does not currently show advertising. If we add ads in future, we will update this policy before they go live and ask for your consent before any advertising cookies are used.

How the news is produced

Each day an automated process reads public news feeds from official company sources, public SEC EDGAR filings and publisher headline feeds. Summaries of official announcements and filings are written by an AI model accessed through OpenRouter. This process handles only publicly available article text. No visitor data is involved or sent to any AI service.

Links to other websites

Stories link to the original publishers and companies, and our menu links to worksmarto.com and architect.worksmarto.com. When you follow a link you leave this site, and the other site’s own privacy policy applies.

Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and keep the correspondence for up to 24 months unless you ask us to delete it sooner. If you use the contact form on worksmarto.com, the worksmarto.com Privacy Policy applies.

Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent personal data. We use these providers, each acting on our instructions or under its own terms:

  • Google LLC: Google Analytics (only with your consent) and Google Fonts.
  • GitHub, Inc.: website hosting (GitHub Pages).

How long we keep it

Google Analytics data is kept for 2 months and is then deleted automatically by Google. We view it only inside Google Analytics and do not export or store it separately. Emails are kept as described below. Hosting logs are kept according to GitHub’s policy.

International transfers

Google LLC and GitHub, Inc. are based in the United States, so data described above is processed outside India and the European Economic Area. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has recognised as providing an adequate level of protection. Both companies describe their safeguards for international transfers in their privacy statements.

Your rights

If you are in India, the DPDP Act gives you the right to access a summary of the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to nominate another person to exercise your rights, and to raise a grievance with us.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR gives you the right to access, correct or erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

You can withdraw analytics consent at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer. Analytics data is held by Google in aggregated form and we cannot usually link it to you, so we will normally hold no personal data about you unless you have emailed us. To exercise any right, write to hello@worksmarto.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Grievance officer

Grievance Officer: Solo Founder of Worksmarto, hello@worksmarto.com.

Children

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we add features that process personal data, such as advertising or a newsletter, we will update this policy before they go live and change the date at the top. Material changes will be flagged on the site.
